The Institutional Logic

机 构 的 脚 印
01
Why blocks exist · 为什么会有订单块

Price Leaves Footprints

S.01

Institutions Cannot Hide机构藏不住自己

Concept 观念

A retail order of $1,000 is nothing — the market absorbs it instantly. An institutional order of $50,000,000 is a different animal: it cannot be filled at one price without moving the market against itself. So institutions work their size in stages, over time. 散户一千美元的订单什么都不是 —— 市场瞬间就消化了。机构五千万美元的订单是另一种生物：它不可能在一个价位全部成交，否则会把价格推向对自己不利的方向。所以机构必须分批、分阶段地慢慢吃进仓位。

They accumulate inside a quiet range — buying while everyone else is bored — and then they need price to leave. The moment the real move starts, the range they built becomes a leftover: a block of resting orders, still sitting exactly where the institution used to work. 他们在安静的区间里吸筹 —— 在所有人都觉得无聊的时候买入 —— 然后他们需要价格离开。真正的行情启动的那一刻，他们建仓的区间就变成了一件残留物：一堆仍然停在那里的挂单，就在机构当初干活的位置上。

  • The move away is the reveal — a violent one-way candle tells you a large player just finished working a position.离开的那段行情就是证据 —— 一根暴力的单边 K 线告诉你，大户刚刚完成建仓。
  • The base before it is the block — the orders that never got filled still rest there.之前的横盘带就是订单块 —— 没成交完的挂单还留在那里。
  • Unfilled orders pull price back — this is exactly why zones get retested.未成交的挂单会把价格拉回来 —— 这就是供需区会被回测的原因。
// A zone is not a drawing. It is the shadow of real order flow. 供需区不是画出来的，它是真实订单流的影子。
S.02

The Imbalance Is the Footprint失衡就是脚印

How to see it 怎么看

Find the fastest, most one-sided move on your chart. That move is the footprint. The consolidation that preceded it is the zone. The two always come as a pair — you cannot have one without the other. 去找图表上最快、最单边的那段行情。那段行情就是脚印；它之前的横盘整理就是供需区。这两者永远成对出现 —— 不可能只有一个而没有另一个。

Why does it keep working? When price returns to that base, the unfilled orders still sit there, and the traders who missed the move are waiting to join it. Both forces push price away from the zone again — that is the whole edge, and it is mechanical, not mystical. 为什么它反复有效？当价格回到横盘带，没成交的挂单还在那里，而错过行情的交易者也正等着上车。两股力量都会再次把价格从区间推开 —— 这就是全部优势所在，它是机械的，不是玄学。

// No imbalance before it, no zone — just an ordinary range. 如果前面没有失衡的行情，那就没有供需区，只是一个普通区间。

Buyers' territory · 买方领地

The Base Before the Drop

S.03

Anatomy of a Demand Zone需求区的解剖

Long setup 做多结构

A demand zone sits below price: a horizontal band where price stopped falling, built a small base, and then exploded upward. Three ingredients, always in this order. 需求区位于价格下方：一条水平的带状区域，价格在这里停止下跌、搭出一个小平台、然后向上爆发。三个要素，顺序永远如此。

  • The drop — price falls fast, often with momentum and expanding range.急跌 —— 价格快速下跌，往往带着动能和放大的波动。
  • The base — a tight, quiet consolidation (3–7 candles) right before the reversal. This band is the zone.横盘带 —— 反转前一段紧凑、安静的整理（3–7 根 K 线）。这条带子就是供需区。
  • The ignition — one powerful bull candle that breaks out of the base and never looks back.起爆点 —— 一根有力的阳线突破横盘带，头也不回。
// The zone is the base, not the low of the drop. Mark the whole band, top to bottom. 供需区是横盘带本身，不是急跌的最低点。把整条带子从上到下都标出来。
S.04

The Ignition Candle起爆点

Signature 签名

The ignition candle is the signature of a real zone. A slow drift up is a sign of balance; a single candle that rips through the base is a sign of force. The more violent the ignition, the more orders were stacked underneath — and the stronger the zone. 起爆点是真实供需区的签名。慢慢爬升代表均衡；一根直接撕裂横盘带的 K 线代表力量。起爆越暴力，底下堆积的订单就越多 —— 供需区就越强。

Freshness matters. A zone that has never been touched since it formed is at full strength. Every retest drains a little of the fuel — treat the first touch as the best trade, the second as good, and anything beyond as leftovers. 新鲜度很重要。形成之后从未被触碰过的供需区是满血状态。每一次回测都会消耗一点燃料 —— 第一次触碰是最好的交易，第二次还不错，再往后就只是残羹剩饭。

// Fresh zone → full strength. Old, tapped zone → cheap trap. 新鲜的区 = 满血；又老又被反复触碰的区 = 便宜的陷阱。

03
Sellers' territory · 卖方领地

The Base Before the Fall

S.05

Anatomy of a Supply Zone供应区的解剖

Short setup 做空结构

A supply zone is the mirror image: a band where price stopped climbing, built a base, and then collapsed. Institutions distributed longs there, and the leftovers still hang above the market like a ceiling. 供应区是镜像版本：一条价格停止爬升、搭出平台、然后崩落的带状区域。机构在这里派发多头仓位，没成交的挂单仍然像天花板一样悬在市场上方。

  • The rally — price climbs with force into the area.急涨 —— 价格带着力量冲进这个区域。
  • The base — tight consolidation at the top; this band is the zone.横盘带 —— 顶部紧凑的整理；这条带子就是供需区。
  • The ignition — a violent bear candle breaks the base downward.起爆 —— 一根暴力的阴线向下砸穿横盘带。
// Same recipe as demand, flipped upside down. 和需求区同一个配方，上下颠倒而已。
S.06

Freshness & Strength新鲜度与强度

Ranking 排名

Zones are consumable. Every touch removes resting orders — some get filled, some get scared away. A supply zone hit five times is a supply zone that has already sold most of its fuel. 供需区是会消耗的。每一次触碰都会移除一部分挂单 —— 有些成交了，有些被吓跑了。被打了五次的供应区，燃料早就卖得差不多了。

Rank your zones by freshness and by the violence of their ignition. Fresh + violent = institutional grade. Old + repeatedly tapped = noise. Only trade the top of the ranking. 用新鲜度和起爆的暴力程度给供需区排名。又新鲜又暴力 = 机构级；又老又被反复触碰 = 噪音。只交易排名最靠前的。

// You are not looking for more zones. You are looking for the best one on the chart right now. 你不是在找更多供需区，你是在找当下图表上最好的那一个。

Zone vs Line

区 不 是 线
04
A band, not a level · 一条带子，不是一个点

Zones Absorb, Lines Describe

S.07

A Line Is a Wish, a Zone Is a Fact线是愿望，区是事实

Key difference 关键区别

Support and resistance are drawn at the extreme of a wick — a single price level. Zones are drawn as the whole base — a band with thickness. That thickness is not sloppiness; it is where the orders actually live. 支撑阻力画在影线的极端 —— 一个单一价位。供需区画成整个横盘带 —— 一条有厚度的带子。这个厚度不是画得随便，而是挂单真正所在的地方。

Watch what happens on retest. Price often pierces the S/R line by a few points before reversing — and every trader who placed a stop exactly on the line gets hunted. A zone absorbs that overshoot inside its band, so entries and stops behave as planned. 看看回测时发生了什么。价格常常先刺穿支撑阻力线几个点再反转 —— 而每一个把止损刚好放在线上的交易者都会被扫掉。供需区的厚度把这种过冲吸收在带子内部，进场和止损才按计划运作。

// Zone in, zone out: enter inside the band, stop beyond it. 区间进、区间出：在带子内部进场，在带子之外放止损。

Let the Zone Come to You

S.08

RTB — Return To Base回测进场

The entry 核心进场法

You never have to buy the breakout. After a zone forms and price runs away, wait for it to come back. The return is the setup — the reaction inside the zone is the entry. 你从来不需要追突破。供需区形成、价格跑远之后，等它回来。回来就是 setup，区间内部的反应 K 线就是进场点。

  • Entry — on the reaction candle inside the zone, or on the break of its high (demand) / low (supply).进场 —— 在区间内的反应 K 线上，或突破该反应 K 线的高点（需求）/低点（供应）时。
  • Stop — beyond the zone with a small buffer. Never inside it.止损 —— 区间之外加一点缓冲。绝不放区间里面。
  • Target — the opposite end of the move that left the zone, or the next zone beyond.目标 —— 离开区间那波行情的另一端，或再往下的下一个供需区。
  • R:R — require at least 1:2 before the trade exists.盈亏比 —— 至少 1:2，这笔交易才存在。
// Patience is the entry. The zone will come to you — or it won't, and that is fine. 耐心就是进场。区间会来找你 —— 如果不来，那也没关系。
S.09

The RTB Checklist回测检查清单

Before you click 点击之前

Before you click, run the list. A zone that fails any line is a zone you skip. 点下去之前，先跑一遍清单。任何一条不过关的供需区，都是你要跳过的区。

  • Fresh? — first or second touch only.新鲜吗？ —— 只做第一、第二次触碰。
  • Violent ignition? — the move away was fast and one-sided.起爆暴力吗？ —— 离开的行情又快又单边。
  • Reaction? — price slows or reverses inside the zone before you enter.有反应吗？ —— 进场前价格在区间内减速或反转。
  • Stop clear? — a defined level beyond the zone, not inside the noise.止损清晰吗？ —— 区间外明确的位置，不在噪音里。
  • 1:2 minimum? — if not, skip. There will be another.至少 1:2 吗？ —— 不够就跳过，还会有下一笔。

A Broken Zone Is a Message

S.10

Breakout Means the Orders Are Gone突破 = 订单没了

Invalidation 失效

A zone is a pile of resting orders. When price closes through the whole band and keeps going, that pile is gone — filled, or blown through. The zone has no fuel left, and trading it again is trading a corpse. 供需区是一堆挂单。当价格收盘穿过整条带子并且继续走，那堆挂单就没了 —— 要么成交了，要么被砸穿了。区间已经没有燃料，再去做它就是做一具尸体。

  • Close, not wick — a wick through is a test; a close through is a verdict.看收盘，不看影线 —— 影线穿过是测试，收盘穿过是判决。
  • Follow-through — one candle beyond is nothing; two or three in the same direction is confirmation.看持续性 —— 一根 K 线穿过去不算数，两三根同方向才算确认。
  • Don't chase, don't average — the zone is dead; adding to a losing zone trade doubles the mistake.不追、不加仓 —— 区间已死；在亏钱的区间单上加仓，是把错误翻倍。
// The invalidation is part of the trade: your stop beyond the zone is the price of admission. 失效点本身就是交易的一部分：区间外的止损就是入场费。
S.11

Role Reversal角色互换

The flip 反转

When a demand zone breaks, it does not disappear. It becomes the supply zone for the next move — the sellers who were trapped in their shorts get their exit, and the trapped longs become overhead supply. 需求区被跌破后不会消失。它会变成下一波行情的供应区 —— 之前被套的空头在这里平仓离场，被套的多头变成头顶的抛压。

This is why the same band of prices keeps working: first as demand, then as supply, then back again. Mark both sides of the reversal before it happens, and you will be waiting where the crowd is forced to act. 这就是为什么同一段价格带反复有效：先是需求，后是供应，然后再反过来。在反转发生之前就把两边的角色都标好，你就会站在人群被迫行动的位置等着。

// Broken support is resistance — the oldest rule in the book, now with a reason. 跌破的支撑变阻力 —— 最古老的规则，现在有了原因。

Zones Are Stronger With the Tide

S.12

With-Trend RTB vs Counter-Trend顺势回测 vs 逆势回测

Context 背景

A demand zone on the daily that sits inside a weekly uptrend is a gift. A demand zone that forms against the weekly trend is a knife — it works, but it works less often, and its failures are violent. 日线需求区落在周线上涨趋势里，那是一份礼物。逆着周线趋势形成的需求区是一把刀 —— 它也能用，但胜率更低，而且失败的时候很惨烈。

  • With trend — RTB in the direction of the higher timeframe; zone and trend agree.顺势 —— 顺着大周期方向的回测进场；供需区与趋势方向一致。
  • Counter trend — scalp-only, smaller size, faster targets; the zone is fighting the tide.逆势 —— 只做短线、减仓、目标放近；供需区在逆水行舟。
  • Confluence — zone + trend + HTF structure or a major level in the same band: the odds stack.共振 —— 供需区 + 趋势 + 大周期结构或重要价位落在同一条带子：胜率叠加。
// Trade zones with the trend. Study them against it. 顺着趋势交易供需区；逆势的只拿来研究。
S.13

The Confluence Checklist共振清单

Permission 许可

The zone is the location; the trend is the permission. A beautiful zone in the wrong direction is a beautiful trap. 供需区是地点，趋势是许可。方向不对的漂亮供需区，是漂亮的陷阱。

  • HTF trend up + demand zone — long bias, full size allowed.大周期向上 + 需求区 —— 做多偏向，可以给足仓位。
  • HTF trend down + supply zone — short bias, full size allowed.大周期向下 + 供应区 —— 做空偏向，可以给足仓位。
  • Zone + HTF structure / major S/R in the same band — high quality, worth waiting for.供需区 + 大周期结构/关键价位在同一带子 —— 高品质，值得等。
  • No confluence — reduce size or skip entirely.没有共振 —— 减仓或直接跳过。

The Zone Is Not the Problem — You Are

S.14

Mistakes That Kill Zone Trading致命错误

Avoid 避开

Every blown zone account follows the same script. Read both columns and recognise yourself in the left one. 每一个爆掉的供需区帐户都遵循同一个剧本。把两栏都读一遍，然后在左边那栏认出你自己。

✕ Chasing the Breakout · 追突破
  1. Price rips away from the zone and you buy the third candle in价格离开区间后，你在第三根 K 线上追进去
  2. The stop has to sit far away, so risk explodes止损被迫放得很远，风险爆炸
  3. Price retests the zone and you are stopped before it bounces价格回测区间时，你已经在反弹前被扫掉
→ Worst entry, worst stop, worst timing.
最差的进场、最差的止损、最差的时机。
✓ Waiting for RTB · 等回测
  1. Zone forms, price runs, you mark the zone and wait区间形成，价格跑远，你标好区间然后等
  2. Price returns, a reaction appears, you enter with a tight stop价格回来，反应出现，你用紧贴的止损进场
  3. Stop beyond the zone, target the swing — risk fixed止损在区外，目标看前高 —— 风险固定
→ Best entry, best stop, best timing.
最好的进场、最好的止损、最好的时机。
  • Trading every zone — most zones are junk. Fresh, violent, confluent only.每个区都做 —— 大部分供需区是垃圾。只做新鲜、暴力、有共振的。
  • Stop inside the zone — the zone is where price breathes; your stop dies of asphyxiation.止损放区间里 —— 区间是价格呼吸的地方，你的止损会被憋死。
  • Averaging into a broken zone — doubling down on a corpse.在失效的区间上加仓 —— 对着一具尸体加倍下注。
  • Moving the stop to "give it room" — that is not room, that is a new, unknown risk.把止损移开「给它一点空间」 —— 那不是空间，那是新的未知风险。
