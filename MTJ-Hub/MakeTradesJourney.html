Who we are · 关于我们

We teach the journey,
not the shortcut.我们教的是一段旅程，不是捷径。

Make Trades Journey is a trading education community built by XRs Trading Lab. We don't sell signals and we don't promise returns. We build traders — one concept, one chart, one disciplined decision at a time. Every module here is bilingual, visual, and built to be understood by someone who has never opened a chart before. Make Trades Journey 是由 XRs Trading Lab 打造的交易教育社群。我们不卖信号，也不承诺收益。我们培养交易者 —— 一个概念、一张图、一个有纪律的决定，慢慢积累。这里的每一课都是中英双语、图解为主，让从未看过图表的人也能读懂。

01 / STRUCTURE

A Real Curriculum

系统化课程

02 / BILINGUAL

English & 中文

中英双语教学

Every explanation appears in both languages, side by side. Learn the concept in the language you think in, and the terminology in the language the market speaks.

03 / VISUAL

Charts, Not Walls of Text

图解式学习

Live-rendered candles, patterns, levels and Fibonacci grids are drawn right in the page — so you see the setup instead of memorising a definition.

04 / RISK FIRST

Risk Before Reward

风控优先

Position sizing, stop placement and the honest limits of any edge come first. A high score on a backtest is not a promise — and we say so, loudly.

Trendlines & Channels
