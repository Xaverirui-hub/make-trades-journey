A.01

Technical Analysis技术分析

The chart看图
trend趋势

Technical analysis reads the price chart itself. The idea: everything that matters is already reflected in price, so patterns tend to repeat. You study candles, trends, support & resistance, and indicators to find your entries.技术分析研究的是价格图表本身。核心理念：所有信息都已反映在价格里，所以形态会重复。你透过蜡烛、趋势、支撑阻力和指标来寻找进场点。

Tools 常用工具：candlestick patterns 蜡烛形态support/resistance 支撑阻力 moving averages 均线 Fibonacci RSI / MACD.

// Suits you if适合你如果：you love charts and want clear entry/exit rules 你喜欢看图，想要明确的进出场规则（偏短线）。

Fundamental Analysis基本面分析

The economy看经济
Interest Rates利率
Inflation通胀
GDP经济增长
Employment就业
News & Politics新闻·政治
Supply & Demand 供需→Currency Value货币价值→Price价格

Fundamental analysis looks at the real-world forces — economic, social and political - that change a currency's supply and demand. Strong economy and rising interest rates tend to strengthen a currency; weak data tends to weaken it.基本面分析关注影响货币供需的现实力量——经济、社会与政治因素。经济强劲、利率上升往往令货币走强；数据疲弱则往往走弱。

Watch 关注：central-bank decisions 央行决议 · inflation & jobs reports 通胀与就业数据 · GDP · major news 重大新闻.

// Suits you if适合你如果：you enjoy economics and news, and think in weeks-to-months 你喜欢研究经济与新闻，偏中长线思考。

How the Crowd Feels群 众 的 感 受

A.03

Sentiment Analysis情绪分析

The crowd看人心
▲ Bulls多头 62%Bears空头 38% ▼
Fear & Greed · who is positioned where 恐惧与贪婪 · 多空持仓分布

Sentiment analysis isn't about the numbers - it's about how traders feel and where the crowd is positioned. When almost everyone is on one side, the market can be primed to turn. Reading fear and greed helps you avoid following the herd off a cliff.情绪分析看的不是数字，而是交易者的感受与人群的持仓方向。当几乎所有人都站在同一边，市场往往就要反转。读懂恐惧与贪婪，能帮你避免盲目跟随人群。

Look at 参考：market positioning 多空持仓 · Fear & Greed index 恐惧贪婪指数 · news & social buzz 新闻与社群情绪.

// Suits you if适合你如果：you like reading the mood and thinking like a contrarian 你善于读情绪，喜欢逆向思考。

You don't have to pick just one你不必只选一种

Think of them as three languages the market speaks. Many traders lead with one (often technical) and use the others to confirm: technicals for the entry, fundamentals for the direction, sentiment for the timing of turns.把它们想成市场说的三种语言。许多交易者以一种为主(常是技术面)，用另外两种来验证：技术面找进场，基本面定方向，情绪面判断反转时机。
