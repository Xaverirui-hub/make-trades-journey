Part 01 · 第一部分

The Math of Loss

亏 损 的 数 学
01
Arithmetic · 算术

Losses Don't Hurt Evenly

R.01

The Asymmetry of Loss亏损的不对称

Critical 关键

Lose 10% and you need 11.1% to get back. Lose 50% and you need 100%. Lose 90% and you need 900%. 亏 10%，要赚 11.1% 才回本；亏 50%，要赚 100%；亏 90%，要赚 900%。

The damage curve is not a straight line - it bends upward sharply. Past roughly 30%, every further loss costs disproportionately more to undo. This single fact is the reason position size matters more than entry quality. 伤害曲线不是直线，它会急速向上弯。过了大约 30% 之后，每多亏一点，要补回来的代价就不成比例地暴增。这一个事实，就是「仓位比进场更重要」的原因。

Gain needed to break even=Loss %÷( 1 − Loss % )
回本所需涨幅 = 亏损% ÷ (1 − 亏损%)
// A 50% drawdown does not need a 50% recovery. It needs a double. 50% 的回撤不是赚 50% 就好，是要翻一倍。
R.02

Losing Streaks Are Normal连亏是必然，不是意外

Expect it 要有预期

With a 50% win rate, a run of 5 losses in a row is close to certain over 100 trades. A run of 7 is common. These are not signs your system broke - they are what randomness looks like. 胜率 50% 的系统，在 100 笔交易里出现连亏 5 次几乎是必然，连亏 7 次也很常见。这不代表你的系统坏了 —— 这就是随机性本来的样子。

So the real question is never "will I have a losing streak?" It is "will my account still be tradeable after one?" At 1% risk, ten straight losses leave you at 90.4% - annoying, survivable. At 10% risk, the same streak leaves 34.9%, and you now need +187% to recover. 所以真正的问题从来不是「我会不会连亏」，而是「连亏之后我的帐户还能不能打」。1% 风险连亏十次剩 90.4%，难受但活着；10% 风险连亏十次剩 34.9%，要再赚 187% 才回得来。

  • 1% risk — 10 losses → 90.4% left, needs +10.6% to recover连亏 10 次剩 90.4%，赚 10.6% 回本 — 一週正常交易就补得回来
  • 3% risk — 10 losses → 73.7% left, needs +35.6% to recover连亏 10 次剩 73.7%，要赚 35.6% 才回本 — 开始难了
  • 5% risk — 10 losses → 59.9% left, needs +67.0% to recover连亏 10 次剩 59.9%，要赚 67% 才回本 — 心态多半已经崩了
  • 10% risk — 10 losses → 34.9% left, needs +186.8% to recover连亏 10 次剩 34.9%，要赚 186.8% 才回本 — 实际上已经结束了
// Size for the streak you will definitely get, not the trade you're looking at right now. 仓位要为「一定会来的连亏」设计，不是为「眼前这一单」设计。

02
Lots & pip value · 手数与点值

What a Lot Actually Costs

R.03

Lot Sizes & Pip Value手数与点值

Foundation 基础

A lot is not a measure of "how big" — it is a measure of how much each point of movement costs you. That is the only thing that matters for risk. 手数不是在描述「多大」，它是在描述价格每动一点你要付多少钱。对风控而言，这是唯一重要的事。

  • Forex — 1.00 lot = 100,000 units of the base currency. For EURUSD, 1 pip (0.0001) = $10.外汇：1 标准手 = 100,000 单位基础货币。EURUSD 每 1 pip（0.0001）= $10。
  • Gold XAUUSD — 1.00 lot = 100 ounces. A $1.00 price move = $100. This is why gold feels violent.黄金 XAUUSD：1 标准手 = 100 盎司。价格每动 $1 = $100。这就是黄金感觉特别凶的原因。
  • Mini / Micro — 0.10 lot is one tenth, 0.01 lot is one hundredth. Same math, smaller numbers.迷你手 0.10 是十分之一，微型手 0.01 是百分之一。算法一样，数字小十倍百倍。
// Specs differ between brokers, especially for metals and indices. Confirm yours before sizing. 点值与合约规格因券商而异（尤其金属与指数），算仓位前请先确认你的规格。
R.04

The Order Most People Get Backwards大部分人顺序是反的

The core 核心

This is the single most important idea in the module. Most traders decide the lot size first and let the risk fall wherever it lands. That means their risk is unknown on every single trade. 这是整课最重要的观念。大部分交易者先决定手数，然后风险就随便落在哪里 —— 等于每一单的风险都是未知的。

✕ Backwards · 错误顺序
  1. "I'll go 0.5 lots this time"「这次下 0.5 手好了」
  2. Enter the trade进场
  3. Put the stop wherever looks fine止损随便找个看起来还行的地方放
→ Risk is unknown. Could be $30, could be $900.
风险是未知的，可能 $30，也可能 $900。
✓ Correct · 正确顺序
  1. Fix the risk first - 1% of balance先定风险金额 —— 帐户的 1%
  2. Place the stop where the idea is proven wrong把止损放在「分析被证明错误」的位置
  3. Calculate the lot size from those two用前两者反推手数
→ Risk is fixed. Lot size is an output.
风险是固定的，手数是算出来的结果。
STEP 01

Fix the Risk

先定风险金额

Decide the money before you look at the chart. 1% of a $5,000 account is $50 - that number does not change because a setup "looks good".

$5,000 × 1% = $50
STEP 02

Place the Stop

再定止损位置

Put it where your analysis is invalidated - beyond structure, not at a round dollar figure you feel comfortable with.

2650.00 → 2645.00 = $5.00
STEP 03

Derive the Lots

最后算出手数

Now the position size is simply arithmetic. There is no judgement left in this step - which is exactly the point.

$50 ÷ $500 = 0.10 lots
Lot Size=Risk Amount÷( Stop Distance × Value per Point per Lot )
手数 = 风险金额 ÷ (止损距离 × 每手每点价值)
// Worked: XAUUSD, stop $5.00 wide. One lot moves $100 per $1 → $500 risk per lot. $50 ÷ $500 = 0.10 lots. 黄金例：止损 $5.00 宽，1 手每 $1 = $100 → 每手风险 $500。$50 ÷ $500 = 0.10 手。

Where the Stop Goes

止 损 放 哪
03
Invalidation · 无效点

A Stop Is a Statement, Not a Budget

R.05

The Stop Answers One Question止损只回答一个问题

Concept 观念

The stop does not answer "how much am I willing to lose?" — the risk % already answered that. The stop answers "at what price is my analysis simply wrong?" 止损回答的不是「我愿意亏多少」—— 那个问题风险%已经回答了。止损回答的是「价格到哪里，我的分析就是错的？」

  • Below the swing low (for longs) - if price takes out the low that formed your setup, the setup is gone.做多放在摆动低点下方 —— 形成这个 setup 的低点被跌破，setup 就不存在了。
  • Beyond structure — outside the range, the neckline, the order block, the trendline you traded.放在结构之外 —— 区间外、颈线外、订单块外、你依据的趋势线之外。
  • Add a buffer — never place it exactly on the obvious level. That is where stop hunts live.加缓冲 —— 不要贴着明显的价位放，那里正是猎杀止损的地方。
  • Use ATR as a sanity check — if the stop is tighter than ~1 ATR, normal noise will take you out.用 ATR 检查 —— 止损若比 1 ATR 还紧，正常波动就会把你扫掉。
// If a correct stop makes the position too small to be worth it, the trade is too expensive. Skip it. 如果正确的止损让仓位小到没意义，那是这笔交易太贵了。放弃它，不要改止损。
R.06

Two Ways to Get It Wrong两种错误

Common 常见
✕ Too Tight · 太紧
  1. Stopped out by normal noise, then price goes your way被正常波动扫掉，然后价格照你的方向走
  2. Feels like the market is hunting you - it isn't, the stop was inside the noise感觉市场在针对你 —— 不是，是止损放在噪音范围内
  3. Leads to revenge re-entries at worse prices导致在更差的价位报复性重新进场
→ High win-rate illusion, death by a thousand cuts.
看似能扛，实际被凌迟。
✕ Too Wide · 太宽
  1. One loss wipes out several wins一次亏损吃掉好几次获利
  2. Or the correct lot size becomes so small the trade is pointless或正确手数小到这笔交易根本没意义
  3. Often a disguised refusal to accept being wrong通常是「不愿承认自己错了」的伪装
→ R:R collapses, expectancy goes negative.
盈亏比崩坏，期望值变负。
// The stop is set by the chart. The lot size adapts to it. Never the other way round. 止损由图表决定，手数去配合它 —— 绝不能反过来。
Part 04 · 第四部分

R:R & Expectancy

盈 亏 比 与 期 望 值
04
Thinking in R · 用 R 思考

Win Rate Alone Means Nothing

R.07

Think in R, Not Dollars用 R 思考，不用金额

Mindset 思维

1R = your risk on that trade. If you risk $50 and make $100, you made 2R. Thinking in R makes results comparable across different account sizes and different instruments - and it strips the emotion out of the number. 1R = 你这笔的风险。risk $50 赚 $100，就是赚了 2R。用 R 思考，不同帐户规模、不同品种的结果才能互相比较，而且能把情绪从数字里抽掉。

Break-even win rate=1÷( 1 + R:R )
打平所需胜率 = 1 ÷ (1 + 盈亏比)
  • 1 : 1 — needs more than 50% win rate just to break even1:1 —— 胜率要超过 50% 才打平
  • 1 : 2 — needs only 33.3%1:2 —— 只需要 33.3% 胜率
  • 1 : 3 — needs only 25%1:3 —— 只需要 25% 胜率
  • 1 : 0.5 — needs 66.7%, which almost nobody sustains1:0.5 —— 需要 66.7% 胜率，几乎没人能长期维持
// Being right less often is fine. Being right small and wrong big is not. 对的次数少没关系；对的时候赚得少、错的时候亏得多，才是问题。
R.08

Expectancy - the Only Number That Matters期望值 —— 唯一重要的数字

Decisive 决定性

Expectancy tells you what one average trade is worth. Everything else - win rate, R:R, how good a setup "feels" — only matters through this one number. 期望值告诉你「平均一笔交易值多少钱」。其他所有东西 —— 胜率、盈亏比、setup 感觉多好 —— 都只透过这个数字才有意义。

Expectancy=( Win% × R )−( Loss% × 1 )
期望值 = (胜率 × 盈亏比) − (败率 × 1)　　单位是 R

System A — 40% win rate at 1:2 → (0.40 × 2) − (0.60 × 1) = +0.20R per trade. Over 100 trades at 1% risk on a $5,000 account, that is roughly +$1,000. 系统 A —— 40% 胜率、1:2 → (0.4×2) − (0.6×1) = +0.20R。$5,000 帐户以 1% 风险做 100 笔，约 +$1,000。

System B — 90% win rate at 1:0.1 → (0.90 × 0.1) − (0.10 × 1) = −0.01R per trade. It wins nine times out of ten and still bleeds the account dry. 系统 B —— 90% 胜率、1:0.1 → (0.9×0.1) − (0.1×1) = −0.01R。十次赢九次，帐户照样慢慢流干。

// This is exactly how martingale and "never take a loss" systems work - a long, beautiful equity curve, then one candle. 这正是马丁格尔与「从不止损」类系统的运作方式 —— 一条漂亮的上升曲线，然后一根 K 线归零。
Part 05 · 第五部分

The Truth About Leverage

杠 杆 的 真 相
05
Misunderstood · 最被误解的东西

Leverage Doesn't Set Your Risk

R.09

Same Lot, Same Loss同样手数，亏一样多

Myth 迷思

Take 0.10 lots of XAUUSD with a $5.00 stop. At 1:20 leverage you lose $50. At 1:100 you lose $50. At 1:500 you lose $50. Leverage changed nothing about the risk. 0.10 手黄金、$5 止损：1:20 亏 $50，1:100 亏 $50，1:500 还是亏 $50。杠杆完全没有改变风险。

What leverage actually changes is the margin — how much of your balance is locked up to hold the position. Higher leverage locks up less, which is convenient. The danger is second-order: because it locks up less, it permits you to open a position far larger than you should. 杠杆真正改变的是保证金 —— 持有这个仓位要冻结你多少余额。杠杆越高冻结越少，很方便。危险是间接的：因为冻结得少，它允许你开出远超过应该开的仓位。

Margin=( Contract Size × Price × Lots )÷Leverage
保证金 = (合约大小 × 价格 × 手数) ÷ 杠杆
  • Risk comes from lot size and stop distance — nothing else.风险来自「手数 × 止损距离」，其他都不是。
  • Margin Level = Equity ÷ Used Margin × 100%. Drop below your broker's stop-out level and positions are closed for you.保证金水平 = 净值 ÷ 已用保证金 × 100%。低于券商的强平线，仓位会被自动平掉。
  • High leverage is not the villain - oversizing is. The leverage just hands you the rope.高杠杆不是元凶，超额下单才是。杠杆只是把绳子递给你。

Three Lines of Defence

三 道 防 线
06
Account level · 帐户层级

Rules You Set Before You're Emotional

R.10

Per Trade → Per Day → Per Month单笔 → 每日 → 每月

Framework 框架

One rule is not enough. A 1% per-trade limit still allows you to lose 15% in a single bad afternoon if you take fifteen trades. Risk needs to be capped at three levels. 只有一条规则不够。单笔 1% 的限制，在你一个下午做十五单的情况下，照样能亏掉 15%。风险必须在三个层级上都设上限。

Line 1 · 第一道
1–2%

Per Trade

单笔风险

Beginners should sit at 0.5–1%. This is the number the calculator uses to derive your lot size.

Line 2 · 第二道
3%

Per Day

每日停损

Or 2–3 losses in a row - whichever comes first. Then you are done for the day. Platform closed.

Line 3 · 第三道
10%

Per Month

最大回撤

Hit this and you stop, review the journal, and go back to demo or half size until the edge is re-established.

R.11

Why the Daily Stop Exists为什么需要每日停损

Psychology 心理

The trade you take right after a loss is, statistically and neurologically, the worst decision you will make all day. Your brain is in recovery mode - it wants the money back, and it wants it back now. Position sizes creep up, entries get sloppier, stops get moved. 亏损之后紧接着的那一单，无论从统计还是生理上看，都是你一整天决策品质最差的一单。大脑进入「讨回来」模式 —— 它想把钱拿回来，而且想马上拿回来。于是仓位悄悄变大、进场变随便、止损开始被移动。

A daily stop is not about the money. It is the calm version of you protecting the tilted version of you, written down in advance, when nothing was at stake. 每日停损跟钱没什么关系。它是「事前冷静的你」用白纸黑字保护「事后上头的你」 —— 在什么都还没发生的时候先写下来。

  • Reduce size in drawdown — down 5%? Halve the risk % until you're back.回撤时降仓 —— 亏了 5%？把风险%减半，直到把它赚回来。
  • Never increase size to recover — that is the mechanism behind almost every blown account.绝不为了回本而加大仓位 —— 几乎每个爆掉的帐户都是这样死的。
  • Write the rules down — a rule you keep in your head is a rule you will renegotiate at the worst moment.把规则写下来 —— 只存在脑子里的规则，会在最糟的时刻被你重新谈判。
