Reading a Quote

B.03

Currency Pairs & Quotes货币对 与 报价

The quote
EUR / USD = 1.10500
EUR · Base 主要货币 被买卖的货币
USD · Quote 对比货币 用来计价的货币
1 EUR = 1.10500 USD

Prices are always quoted in pairs. The first currency is the base (what you buy or sell); the second is the quote (what it's priced in). The number tells you how much quote currency one unit of the base is worth.价格永远以货币对报出。第一个是主要货币(被买卖的)，第二个是对比货币(用来计价的)。数字表示一单位主要货币值多少对比货币。

Corrected

Bid买价 — the price you can sell the base at (the broker buys from you). Ask卖价 — the price you can buy at (the broker sells to you). The Ask is always higher than the Bid, and the gap between them is the Spread — effectively your cost to trade.买价 Bid——你能卖出主要货币的价格(经纪商向你买)。卖价 Ask——你能买入的价格(经纪商卖给你)。卖价永远高于买价，两者之差就是点差 Spread，也就是你的交易成本。

// Spread点差 = Ask 卖价 − Bid 买价 · always ≥ 0 永远为正

Terms & Pips

B.05

Trading Terms做单术语

Vocabulary
Buy · Long ▲买 / 多
看涨——预期主要货币上涨时买入。
Sell · Short ▼卖 / 空
看跌——预期主要货币下跌时卖出。
Lot 手数
买入 / 卖出货币的数量单位，决定仓位大小。
Pip 点数
衡量价格变动的最小基本单位。

Go Long (Buy) when you expect the base to rise; go Short (Sell) when you expect it to fall. A Lot is your position-size unit, and a Pip is the smallest standard price increment.做多(买)预期上涨；做空(卖)预期下跌。手数决定仓位大小，点数是价格变动的最小标准单位。

B.06

Reading Pips点数的解读

Where is a pip?
Most pairs多数货币对
1.10500
4th decimal 第 4 位小数 = 0.0001
JPY pairs日元货币对
110.50
2nd decimal 第 2 位小数 = 0.01

A pip sits at a different decimal place depending on the pair. For most pairs it's the 4th decimal (0.0001); for JPY pairs it's the 2nd decimal (0.01). The tiny last digit some brokers show is a fractional pip (1/10).点数在不同货币对位于不同的小数位。多数货币对是第 4 位小数(0.0001)；日元货币对是第 2 位小数(0.01)。有些经纪商多显示的最后一位是小数点(1/10 点)。

Making Sense of Price

B.07

Profit Calculation获利计算方式

How you earn
Profit 盈亏 = Pips点数 × Pip value每点价值 × Lots手数
▲ Buy / Long买入
Entry 进场 1.1000 → Exit 出场 1.1020
= +20 pips × $10 × 1 lot
= +$200
▼ Sell / Short卖出
Entry 进场 1.1000 → Exit 出场 1.0990
= +10 pips × $10 × 1 lot
= +$100

Your profit is simply how many pips you captured, times the pip value, times your lot size. Buying profits when price rises; selling profits when price falls.盈亏就是你抓到的点数，乘以每点价值，再乘以手数。买入在上涨中获利，卖出在下跌中获利。

// Pip value depends on lot size & pair — $10/pip per standard lot shown here每点价值取决于手数与货币对，此处以标准手 $10/点 为例
B.08

Why Price Moves市场为何涨跌

Supply & demand
PRICE价格 两者之间的拔河 · a tug of war

Price moves because of a constant tug of war between buyers and sellers. When buyers are stronger, price rises; when sellers dominate, it falls. Every candle is a snapshot of who won.价格波动源于买家与卖家之间不断的拔河。买方更强价格上涨，卖方占优价格下跌。每一根蜡烛都是这场较量的结果快照。

B.09

Japanese Candlesticks日式蜡烛图 (K线)

Anatomy

Each candle shows four prices in one shape: the open and close form the body, while the high and low form the wicks (shadows). A green candle closed higher (bullish); a red one closed lower (bearish).每根蜡烛用一个形状显示四个价格：开盘与收盘组成烛体，最高与最低组成影线。绿色收高为上涨，红色收低为下跌。
