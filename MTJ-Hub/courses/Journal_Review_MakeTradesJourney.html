01
Feedback · 回馈

Memory Is a Terrible Record

J.01

What You Remember vs. What Happened你记得的 vs. 实际发生的

Bias 偏差

Without a journal, all you have at the end of the month is one number: the balance. You know whether you made money. You have no idea why. 没有日志，月底你手上只有一个数字：余额。你知道自己有没有赚钱，但完全不知道为什么。

And memory will not fill the gap - it actively distorts it. Three well-documented effects work against you: 记忆填不了这个空缺，而且会主动扭曲它。有三种效应在跟你作对：

  • You over-remember the wins — especially the big ones. The quiet, correct, small losses vanish.你会过度记得赢的单，尤其是大赚的那几笔。那些安静、正确、小额的亏损直接消失。
  • You over-remember the last few trades — a bad week feels like a bad system.你会过度记得最近几笔 —— 一週表现差，就感觉整套系统坏了。
  • You rewrite your reasons afterwards — "I knew that would happen" is almost never true.你会事后重写理由 ——「我就知道会这样」几乎从来不是真的。
// A journal is not paperwork. It is the only instrument you have for measuring yourself. 日志不是行政工作，它是你唯一能用来量测自己的仪器。
J.02

What a Journal Actually Buys You日志到底换来什么

Payoff 回报
  • You find out which setup actually pays. Most traders have one profitable setup and three that quietly bleed. Without data you cannot tell them apart - they all feel the same in the moment.你会发现哪个 setup 真的在赚钱。大部分人有一个赚钱的 setup 和三个默默流血的 —— 没有数据你分不出来，因为当下感觉都一样。
  • You find out when you trade badly. Session, day of week, after a loss, late at night.你会发现自己什么时候交易得差：时段、星期几、亏损之后、深夜。
  • You separate a bad system from a bad run. This is the single most expensive confusion in trading.你能分辨「系统坏了」和「只是运气差」—— 这是交易里代价最高的一种混淆。
  • You get evidence to argue with yourself. When you want to break a rule, the log is the counter-argument.你会有跟自己吵架的证据。当你想破规则时，日志就是反方意见。

Judge the Process, Not the Result

J.04

Kept the Rules? × Won or Lost?守规则？× 赢还是输？

Core idea 核心

A single trade's outcome tells you almost nothing - markets are noisy enough that a good decision can lose and a terrible one can win. What you can judge honestly, on every single trade, is whether you followed your own plan. 单笔交易的结果几乎说明不了什么 —— 市场的随机性足以让好决策亏钱、烂决策赚钱。但有一件事你每一笔都能诚实评断：你有没有照自己的计画做。

✓ KEPT + WIN

Process Win

守 规 则 · 赢

The system worked and you executed it. This is the only quadrant that is genuinely repeatable.

More of this. Nothing to fix.正循环 —— 这才是可复制的获利。
✓ KEPT + LOSS

A Good Loss

守 规 则 · 输

You did everything right and still lost. This is the cost of doing business - it is supposed to happen, often.

Do not change anything. This is not a mistake.好的亏损 —— 什么都不用改，你没做错任何事。
✕ BROKE + WIN

The Dangerous Win

破 规 则 · 赢

You moved the stop, doubled down, or entered without your setup - and got paid for it. The market just rewarded the exact behaviour that will eventually destroy the account.

The most expensive box on this page.最危险的一格 —— 它在奖励那个最终会毁掉你帐户的行为。
✕ BROKE + LOSS

A Deserved Loss

破 规 则 · 输

Painful, but honest. The feedback matches the behaviour, which makes it the easiest kind of mistake to correct.

Painful, but it teaches the right lesson.活该的亏损 —— 但至少诚实，最容易改。

Notice what this reframes. A losing month where every trade sits in the top two boxes is a good month — the process is intact and the edge will show up. A winning month built on the bottom-left box is a warning, not a success. 注意这样重新框架之后的改变：一个亏损的月份，如果每一笔都落在上面两格，那是个好月份 —— 流程完好，优势迟早会显现。而一个靠左下角那格赚来的获利月份，是警讯，不是成功。

// Target: 90%+ of your trades in the "KEPT" column, regardless of win or loss. Chase that number, not the P&L. 目标：不管输赢，90% 以上的交易都落在「守规则」这一栏。去追这个数字，不要追损益。

Daily, Weekly, Monthly

J.05

Three Loops, Three Jobs三个循环，三种任务

SOP 流程

Reviewing at the wrong frequency is worse than not reviewing at all. Look at your stats every day and you will react to noise; look once a year and the lesson arrives too late. 用错误的频率复盘，比不复盘更糟。每天看统计，你会对噪音做反应；一年才看一次，教训来得太晚。

DAILY · 每日
10
minutes 分钟

Record & Rate

记录与评分

  • One sentence: what would I repeat, what would I not一句话：哪里会重做、哪里不会

统计与比较

05
Metrics · 指标

Six Numbers That Matter

J.06

What to Look At, In Order按顺序该看什么

Metrics 指标
  • 1. Rule-following rate — % of trades in the "KEPT" column. If this is below 80%, stop reading the other numbers; they are measuring a system you are not actually trading.1. 守规则比率 —— 落在「守规则」那栏的比例。低于 80% 的话，其他数字不用看了 —— 它们量的是一套你其实没在执行的系统。
  • 2. Expectancy (R per trade) — the headline. Positive means the system pays. This is the number from the risk module.2. 期望值（每笔 R） —— 头条数字。正数代表这套系统是赚的。就是风控课里那个数字。
  • 3. Expectancy by setup — where the truth hides. Usually one setup carries everything and one is a slow leak.3. 按 setup 分类的期望值 —— 真相藏在这里。通常是一个 setup 扛起全部，另一个在慢慢漏水。
  • 4. Max drawdown in R — compare against the cap you set in the risk module. If you have already exceeded it, the problem is sizing, not strategy.4. 以 R 计的最大回撤 —— 拿去跟风控课里设的上限比。如果已经超过，问题在仓位不在策略。
  • 5. Max losing streak — reality-check your risk %. Whatever your longest streak is, assume the next one is longer.5. 最大连亏次数 —— 用来检验你的风险%。不管你目前最长连亏是几次，都要假设下一次会更长。
  • 6. Profit factor — gross profit ÷ gross loss. Below 1.0 you are losing; a healthy discretionary system usually sits between 1.2 and 2.0.6. 获利因子 —— 总获利 ÷ 总亏损。低于 1.0 就是在亏；一套健康的主观交易系统通常落在 1.2 到 2.0 之间。
// Win rate is deliberately not on this list. On its own it tells you nothing - see the expectancy section of the risk module. 胜率刻意不在这张清单上。单看胜率什么都说明不了 —— 参考风控课的期望值那一段。
J.07

Find the Setup That Actually Pays找出真正在赚钱的那个 setup

Highest ROI 最有价值

This single breakdown is usually worth more than any new indicator you will ever add. In the example above the trader's overall expectancy is barely positive - but that average is hiding a strong setup and a genuinely bad one. Cutting the worst setup alone would roughly double the account's performance without learning anything new. 这一张分类表，通常比你之后会加的任何新指标都值钱。上图中这位交易者的整体期望值勉强为正 —— 但那个平均值同时藏着一个很强的 setup 和一个真的很差的。光是砍掉最差的那个，帐户表现大约就能翻倍，而且完全不需要学任何新东西。

Part 06 · 第六部分

When to Change

何 时 该 改 系 统
06
Sample size · 样本数

Bad Run or Bad System?

J.08

The Sample Size Trap样本数陷阱

Critical 关键

Take a system whose true win rate is exactly 50%. Trade it 20 times and, by chance alone, you could reasonably observe anything from 28% to 72%. At 50 trades the range narrows to roughly 36–64%. You need around 200 trades before the observed number sits reliably close to the truth. 假设一套系统的真实胜率恰好是 50%。只做 20 笔的话，单纯因为运气，你观察到的胜率可能落在 28% 到 72% 之间。做到 50 笔，范围缩到大约 36–64%。要到大约 200 笔，观察值才会稳定地贴近真相。

This is why changing your system after ten bad trades is not discipline - it is noise-chasing. And it is also why a great first month proves nothing. 这就是为什么「十笔亏损后就改系统」不是纪律，是在追噪音。同样地，第一个月表现很好，也证明不了任何事。

  • Under 30 trades — record only. Change nothing about the system.不足 30 笔 —— 只记录，系统一个字都别改。
  • 30–100 trades — you may adjust execution (entry timing, exits), not the core rules.30–100 笔 —— 可以调整执行细节（进场时机、出场方式），核心规则不动。
  • 100+ trades — now a setup with a clearly negative expectancy can be cut with confidence.100 笔以上 —— 这时候才有底气砍掉期望值明显为负的 setup。
  • Always — a rule-following rate below 80% means the problem is you, not the system. No amount of data fixes that.任何时候 —— 守规则比率低于 80%，代表问题在你不在系统。再多数据也解决不了。
// Change one thing at a time, and write down the date you changed it. Otherwise the next 100 trades measure nothing. 一次只改一件事，并且写下你是哪一天改的。否则接下来那 100 笔什么都量不出来。
