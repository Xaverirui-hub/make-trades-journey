Edge vs luck · 优势与运气

An Edge Is a Statistical Property优势是统计属性，不是感觉

B.01

The Coin-Flip Test掷硬币测试

The core 核心

One winning trade proves nothing. Ten winning trades prove very little - a coin flip can produce either. The only thing that separates an edge from luck is a positive expectation repeated over many trades. 一笔盈利证明不了什么，十笔盈利也证明不了多少 —— 掷硬币也能掷出这种结果。区分优势与运气的，只有一件事：正期望值被反复执行很多次。

Backtesting is how you find out which one you have — before real money answers the question for you. The chart above is the whole argument: the edge and the coin flip are nearly indistinguishable for the first thirty trades, then the law of large numbers quietly separates them. 回测就是用来搞清楚你手里到底是哪一种 —— 在真金白银替你回答之前。上图就是全部论点：前三十笔，优势与掷硬币几乎无法区分，然后大数定律悄悄把两者分开。

  • Edge = a statistical property of a sequence, never of any single trade.优势是「一串交易」的统计属性，从来不是某一笔的属性。
  • Your brain is a pattern machine — it will find "skill" inside pure randomness.大脑是找规律的机器 —— 在纯粹的随机里，它也能找出「技术」。
  • Backtest = a controlled experiment where the only variable is your rule.回测 = 控制变量的实验，唯一的变量是你的规则。
Edge=Expectancy per trade > 0→ repeat → law of large numbers
优势 = 每笔期望值 > 0 → 反复执行 → 大数定律替你完成剩下的
// If your strategy is only "good" in hindsight, you don't have a strategy. 如果策略只在事后看才「好」，你并没有策略。

B.02

What Backtesting Actually Is回测到底是什么

Foundation 基础

A backtest replays history under one fixed set of rules and asks: if I had followed this system, what would have happened? Every entry, exit and risk decision must be defined before the data is seen. 回测用一套固定规则重放历史，问一个问题：如果我当年照这套系统做，会发生什么？每一个进场、出场、风控决定，都必须在看数据之前先定义好。

The output is not profit. The output is evidence — win rate, profit factor, drawdowns - numbers you can hold up against pure randomness and against your own hopes. 回测的产出不是利润，而是证据 —— 胜率、盈利因子、回撤 —— 是可以拿来和纯随机、和你自己的期待对质的数字。

  • Test across market regimes — trends, ranges, high and low volatility.跨市场环境测试 —— 趋势、震荡、高波动、低波动都要覆盖。
  • Walk-forward — train on one period, verify on the next, never the same data twice.前推验证 —— 一段训练、下一段验证，同一份数据绝不用两次。
  • If the edge lives in one specific year, treat it as a coincidence.如果优势只存在于某一年，把它当巧合处理。
// A backtest you cannot fail is not a backtest - it is a screensaver. 不会挂科的回测不是回测，是屏保。
B.03

Lookahead & Survivorship Bias前视偏差与幸存者偏差

Danger 危险

Lookahead bias means your rules quietly use information that did not exist at decision time — tomorrow's close, the final revised GDP figure, the day's high printed after your entry. The backtest sees the future; you never will. 前视偏差，就是你的规则悄悄使用了做决定那一刻还不存在的信息 —— 明天的收盘价、修订后的 GDP 终值、你进场之后才出现的当日高点。回测看得见未来，你永远看不见。

Survivorship bias is the other ghost: testing only on symbols that still exist today, forgetting the ones that got delisted. History that survived is not the same as history that happened. 幸存者偏差是另一个幽灵：只测试今天还活着的品种，忘了那些已经退市的。幸存下来的历史，不等于真实发生过的历史。

  • Iron rule: every input to a decision must be known at that candle's close.铁律：决策用到的每个数据，都必须在该 K 线收盘时已经知道。
  • Close only — never the high or low of the same candle you act on.只用收盘价 —— 绝不用你正在操作的这根 K 线的高低价。
  • Include the dead — delisted instruments belong in the test too.纳入已退市品种 —— 它们也应该出现在测试里。
// When in doubt, ask: could I have known this at 15:00? If no, it doesn't belong in the test. 拿不准就问：下午三点这一刻，我真的知道这个数据吗？不知道，它就不该出现在测试里。

Costs Decide Which Backtests Are Real成本决定哪些回测是真的

B.04

Spread, Commission, Slippage点差、佣金、滑点

Critical 关键

A backtest without costs is a fantasy. Every fill pays the spread, every round trip pays commission, and every stop that gaps pays slippage. On a 1-pip spread, a system that "earns" 5 pips per trade gross actually earns 3. 不含成本的回测是幻想。每一笔成交都付点差，每一次来回都付佣金，每一个跳空成交的止损都付滑点。1 个点差之下，所谓每笔「赚 5 个点」的系统，实际只赚 3 个点。

The smaller your edge per trade, the bigger the share costs eat. A system that nets +0.05R after costs is a real system; one that nets −0.05R is a donation. 每笔优势越小，成本吃掉的份额越大。扣完成本净 +0.05R 的系统是真的系统；净 −0.05R 的是捐款。

  • Spread — you enter at ask, exit at bid; the distance between them is paid twice.点差 —— 买在卖价、卖在买价，一来一回付两次。
  • Commission — fixed per lot; test at your broker's actual rate, not a round number.佣金 —— 按手数固定收取；用你券商真实费率测，别用整数。
  • Slippage — stops fill worse than your test assumes, especially in fast news.滑点 —— 快速行情里止损的成交价，总比你测试假设的差。
  • Rule of thumb — if it dies at +1 pip extra cost, it was never an edge.经验法则 —— 多加 1 个点成本就活不下来的，从来就不是优势。
Net R=Gross R−( spread + commission + slippage ) per trade
净 R = 毛 R − 每笔(点差 + 佣金 + 滑点)
// Add costs from day one. Adding them later is how systems "mysteriously" stop working. 从第一天起就计入成本。事后才补成本，就是系统「莫名其妙失效」的原因。

B.05

Minimum Sample Size最小样本量

Statistics 统计

Thirty trades are a rumor, not a result. With a true 55% win rate, 30 trades will measure anywhere from roughly 37% to 73% — a range that includes both "genius" and "broken". The confidence interval math is unforgiving. 三十笔是传闻，不是结果。真实胜率 55% 的系统，30 笔测出来大概落在37% 到 73% 之间 —— 这个区间里既有「天才」也有「废柴」。置信区间的数学毫不留情。

For a swing or position system, 100–150 trades is the practical minimum; 300+ is respectable; more is better, as long as the trades span different market conditions. 对波段或趋势系统而言，100–150 笔是实际底线；300 笔以上才算体面；越多越好，前提是这些交易跨越不同的市场环境。

  • N = 30 — error ±17.8%: indistinguishable from a coin flip.30 笔 —— 误差 ±17.8%：和掷硬币无法区分。
  • N = 100 — error ±9.7%: the minimum for a first verdict.100 笔 —— 误差 ±9.7%：第一次下结论的最低标准。
  • N = 300 — error ±5.6%: now you have a real opinion.300 笔 —— 误差 ±5.6%：现在你有真正的观点了。
  • N = 1,000 — error ±3.1%: close enough to the truth to act on.1000 笔 —— 误差 ±3.1%：接近真相，可以行动了。
// Fewer than 100 trades and your "edge" is a mood. 不到 100 笔，你的「优势」只是一种情绪。
B.06

The Five Numbers That Matter五个关键数字

Decisive 决定性

Win rate is the most quoted and least useful number. What matters is the whole set - profit factor, expectancy, max drawdown and Sharpe — read together. One metric can lie; five that agree are hard to fool. 胜率是被引用最多、最没用的数字。重要的是完整的一组 —— 盈利因子、期望值、最大回撤、夏普 —— 放在一起读。单一指标会说谎，五个指标一致就很难骗你。

  • Profit Factor = gross profit ÷ gross loss. Above 1.5 is meaningful; below 1.2 is noise.盈利因子 = 毛盈利 ÷ 毛亏损。高于 1.5 有意义，低于 1.2 是噪音。
  • Expectancy = average R per trade. Stable and positive beats large and unstable.期望值 = 每笔平均 R。稳定的小正数，胜过波动剧烈的大数字。
  • Max Drawdown = the deepest hole. Your account must survive it twice in a row.最大回撤 = 最深的一个坑。你的帐户要能连续扛两次。
  • Sharpe = return per unit of volatility. 1+ is decent; 2+ is rare and deserves suspicion.夏普 = 每单位波动换来的收益。1 以上不错，2 以上罕见且值得怀疑。
PF=ΣWins÷ΣLosses·E=(W%×AvgWin)−(L%×AvgLoss)·Sharpe=(R−Rf)÷σ
盈利因子 = 总盈利 ÷ 总亏损　·　期望值 = (胜率×平均盈利) − (败率×平均亏损)　·　夏普 = (收益 − 无风险) ÷ 波动
// A beautiful win rate with a negative expectancy is a subscription to losing. 胜率漂亮但期望值为负，等于订阅了一份亏损套餐。

B.07

The Overfitting Trap过拟合陷阱

Warning 警告

Every parameter you tune is a vote for the past. Optimize enough dials - entry timing, moving-average length, stop distance, time filter - and any random walk will produce a gorgeous equity curve. That is curve-fitting, not discovery. 你每调一个参数，就是给过去投一票。旋钮拧得够多 —— 进场时机、均线长度、止损距离、时间过滤 —— 任何随机游走都能生成一条漂亮的权益曲线。那是拟合曲线，不是发现规律。

The classic tells: a "sweet spot" that is a narrow spike, in-sample profit that degrades out-of-sample, and rules with more parameters than the sample can support. 典型特征：所谓「最优区」是个窄尖峰；样本内利润在样本外缩水；规则参数比样本本身能支撑的还多。

  • Limit parameters — 3 to 5 at most for a first system.限制参数 —— 第一版系统最多 3–5 个。
  • Test neighbours — a real edge is a plateau, not a spike.测试相邻参数 —— 真优势是高原，不是尖峰。
  • Never re-tune on data you already judged — use walk-forward only.绝不在已下过结论的数据上重新调参 —— 只用前推验证。
  • "Too good" is a red flag — if it looks too good, it probably is.「好到不真实」是红旗 —— 看起来太好，那多半就是不真实。
// Overfitting is how a backtest makes you rich on paper and poor in reality. 过拟合就是：回测让你在纸面上暴富，实盘让你在现实中变穷。
B.08

One Strategy vs a Portfolio单一策略 vs 策略组合

Diversify 分散

A single strategy is a single bet on one market behaviour. A portfolio of strategies - different instruments, different logic, different timeframes - smooths the equity curve because each piece reacts differently to the same week. 单一策略，是把所有赌注押在一种市场行为上。策略组合 —— 不同品种、不同逻辑、不同周期 —— 能把权益曲线磨平，因为每个部分对同一周的反应都不一样。

Correlation is the key number. Two strategies that both die in a range market are not diversification - they are the same bet twice. You want edges that are independent, so a drawdown in one is carried by the others. 相关性才是关键数字。两个都在震荡市里死掉的策略不是分散 —— 是同一个赌注押了两次。你要的是互相独立的优势，这样其中一个回撤时，其他能扛住。

  • Different instruments — FX, gold and indices rarely crash together.不同品种 —— 外汇、黄金、指数很少一起崩。
  • Different logic — trend-following + mean-reversion + breakout.不同逻辑 —— 趋势跟踪 + 均值回归 + 突破。
  • Watch correlation in crises — that is when diversification is really tested.危机时刻才看得出相关性 —— 那才是分散的真正考场。
// The portfolio's job is not to make more - it is to make the same with smaller drawdowns. 组合的职责不是赚更多，而是用更小的回撤赚同样的钱。

B.09

Entry, Exit, Risk - in That Order进场、出场、风控 —— 按这个顺序

Framework 框架

An entry without an exit is a hobby. Define where you are wrong (stop), where you take profit (target or exit rule), and how much you risk — before you click. The market will not wait while you improvise. 只有进场没有出场的，是爱好不是系统。在点击之前就定义好哪里算错（止损）、哪里收钱（目标或出场规则）、风险多大（固定%）。市场不会等你临场发挥。

STEP 01

Entry

进场：客观触发 + 环境过滤

A trigger you can describe in one sentence, plus a filter that keeps you out more often than it lets you in.

if close > MA20 and RSI < 70 → long
STEP 02

Exit

出场：失效止损 + 止盈 + 时间止损

Where the idea is invalidated, where you bank profit, and when you leave simply because the trade took too long.

stop = structure · target = 2R · time = 5 days
STEP 03

Risk

风控：单笔固定% + 日线月线上限

Fixed % per trade, capped per day and per month - the numbers from the Risk Management module.

1% / trade · 3% / day · 10% / month
  • Entry — objective trigger + context filter, both written down.进场 —— 客观触发器 + 环境过滤器，全部写下来。
  • Exit — invalidation stop + profit-taking rule + time stop.出场 —— 失效止损 + 止盈规则 + 时间止损。
  • Risk — fixed % per trade, capped daily and monthly.风控 —— 单笔固定%，日线与月线设上限。
  • The one-sentence test — can a stranger execute your system from the written rules alone?一句话测试 —— 陌生人只凭文字规则，能不能执行你的系统？
Rule quality = ( can it be coded? ) × ( can it be followed? )
规则质量 = (能不能写成代码) × (能不能被严格执行)
// A vague rule is a veto you hand to your future self at the worst moment. 模糊的规则，是你在最糟的时刻，送给未来那个自己的否决权。

B.10

The Paper Trading Protocol纸上交易验证流程

Process 流程

Paper trading tests the bridge between your backtest and reality: can you actually follow the rules, fill at the assumed prices, and stay disciplined for 30+ trades? It tests you at least as much as it tests the system. 纸上交易测试的是回测与现实的桥梁：你真的能照规则执行吗？成交价和你假设的一致吗？能坚持 30 笔以上吗？它考你的程度，不亚于考系统。

Then move to live with a token size: one mini lot, real broker, real fills. The goal is not profit - it is to confirm the paper results survive execution, costs and your own psychology. 然后用小到象征性的仓位上实盘：迷你手、真实券商、真实成交。目标不是赚钱 —— 是确认纸上的结果扛得住执行、成本和你自己的心理。

  • Paper criteria — 30–50 trades, live-feed demo, costs included, rules followed to the letter.纸上交割标准 —— 30–50 笔、实盘行情模拟、含成本、规则一字不差。
  • Small live criteria — 20–30 trades, net expectancy still positive, drawdown inside backtest bounds.小仓实盘标准 —— 20–30 笔、净期望仍为正、回撤在回测范围内。
  • Scale up only after both pass — and cap the first live month at half risk.两个阶段都通过才放大 —— 实盘第一个月风险减半。
  • Keep journaling — your live fills are the new data set.坚持记录 —— 你的实盘成交，就是新的数据集。
// If you can't follow the rules on paper, you won't follow them with money. 纸上都守不住的规则，真金白银更守不住。
