TI.01

Three Reasons to Look Beyond三个走出去的理由

Foundation基础

Forex is 24/5 and liquid, but it is one market. When EURUSD is flat, gold may be trending and oil may be collapsing. Trading several instruments means your opportunities do not all switch off at the same time. 外汇 24/5 且流动性好，但它只是一个市场。当欧美死水一潭时，黄金可能正在走趋势，原油可能正在暴跌。多交易几个品种，你的机会就不会在同一时间全部熄火。

  • Diversification — metals, oil and indices move on different drivers, so a quiet forex day is not a wasted day.分散——金属、原油、指数的驱动因素各不相同，外汇安静的一天不会白费。
  • Volatility choice — gold trends, oil spikes, indices grind. Pick the personality that fits your style.波动可选——黄金走趋势、原油打脉冲、指数慢慢磨。挑一个适合你风格的脾气。
  • 24/5 coverage — forex never sleeps, but each instrument peaks at its own hour. A round-the-clock trader always has a market that is awake.24/5 覆盖——外汇不睡觉，但每个品种都有自己的高峰。全天候交易者永远有一个醒着的市场。
// The skills transfer as-is: levels, trends, risk. Only the instrument's personality changes.技巧原样迁移：关键位、趋势、风险。变的只是品种的脾气。

TI.03

Gold — The Safe Haven黄金——避险资产

Haven避险

XAUUSD is gold priced in US dollars — one ounce of gold against the dollar. For centuries gold has been the world's safe haven: when war, crisis or fear hits, money flows into gold. It is also the classic inflation hedge — when paper money loses purchasing power, gold tends to hold its value. XAUUSD 是以美元计价的黄金——一盎司黄金兑美元。几个世纪以来，黄金一直是世界的避险资产：战争、危机、恐慌一来，资金就涌向黄金。它也是经典的通胀对冲——纸币购买力缩水时，黄金倾向于保值。

The most important relationship: gold vs the US dollar. Gold is priced in dollars, so a stronger dollar usually means weaker gold, and a weaker dollar means stronger gold. Watch DXY alongside your gold chart — they behave like a mirror. 最重要的一组关系：黄金 vs 美元。黄金以美元计价，美元走强黄金通常走弱，美元走弱黄金通常走强。看黄金图时同时盯住 DXY（美元指数）——它们像镜子一样相反。

  • Safe haven — geopolitical tension and market fear push gold up.避险——地缘紧张与市场恐慌推升黄金。
  • Inflation hedge — rising CPI and negative real yields support gold.通胀对冲——CPI 上升与负实际利率支撑黄金。
  • Dollar inverse — stronger USD, weaker gold; weaker USD, stronger gold.与美元负相关——美元强黄金弱，美元弱黄金强。
// NFP, CPI and FOMC can move gold dozens of dollars in seconds — the news calendar is part of the gold chart.非农、CPI、FOMC 能在几秒内让黄金波动几十美元——数据日历就是黄金图的一部分。
Gold up, dollar down — the mirror trade, day after day黄金涨、美元跌——日复一日的镜像行情
TI.04

Silver — The Dual Personality白银——双重性格

Dual双属性

XAGUSD is silver priced in dollars. Silver is two metals in one: a precious metal like gold (safe-haven demand) and an industrial metal (solar panels, electronics, batteries). Industrial demand ties silver to the economic cycle more than gold — and makes it far more volatile. XAGUSD 是以美元计价的白银。白银一身兼两职：既是像黄金一样的贵金属（避险需求），又是工业金属（光伏、电子、电池）。工业需求让白银比黄金更贴近经济周期——也因此波动大得多。

One ounce of silver costs a fraction of an ounce of gold, so the same percentage move is a smaller dollar swing — but percentage-wise silver moves harder. The gold/silver ratio (XAU ÷ XAG, historically ~60–80) shows which metal is relatively expensive, and extreme readings tend to mean-revert. 一盎司白银的价格只有黄金的零头，同样的百分比波动在白银上美元幅度更小——但按百分比算，白银摆动更猛。金银比（XAU ÷ XAG，历史约 60–80）显示哪个金属相对更贵，极端读数往往均值回归。

  • Precious + industrial — two demand engines, two sets of drivers.贵金属 + 工业——两台需求引擎，两套驱动因素。
  • More volatile — silver swings a larger percentage on the same news.波动更大——同样的消息，白银百分比波动更剧烈。
  • Gold/silver ratio — the relative-value gauge between the two metals.金银比——衡量两个金属相对贵贱的标尺。
// Silver follows gold's headlines, then adds its own industrial twist. Two charts, one eye on the ratio.白银跟着黄金的消息走，再叠上自己的工业属性。两张图，别忘了看一眼金银比。

WTI vs BrentWTI 与布伦特的区别

Key difference关键差异
WTI美油
USOIL · West Texas Intermediate
The US benchmark, delivered at Cushing, Oklahoma. Reflects North American supply and demand; most sensitive to weekly EIA inventory data.美国基准原油，交割地俄克拉荷马州库欣。反映北美供需，对每周 EIA 库存数据最敏感。
US benchmark美国基准
Brent布油
UKOIL · North Sea Brent
The global benchmark, produced in the North Sea. About two-thirds of world crude is priced off Brent; more sensitive to OPEC decisions and geopolitics.全球基准原油，产自北海。全球约三分之二的原油以布伦特定价；对欧佩克决策与地缘事件更敏感。
Global benchmark全球基准
WTI leads on US data; Brent leads on OPEC and geopolitics美油跟着美国数据走，布油跟着欧佩克与地缘走

Both are the same product — crude oil — but priced at different locations, so a spread between them is normal (Brent usually a little higher). Their daily moves are highly correlated; the dominant driver of each is different: US inventories for WTI, OPEC and geopolitics for Brent. 两者是同一种商品——原油——但定价地点不同，之间存在价差很正常（通常布油略高）。两者日线走势高度相关，但主导各自的驱动不同：WTI 看美国库存，布油看欧佩克与地缘。

// Trade one, watch the other — divergence between WTI and Brent is itself a signal.做其中一个就要盯另一个——WTI 与布油的分叉本身就是信号。
TI.06

What Moves Oil什么在驱动油价

Drivers驱动

Oil is the most politically charged market you will trade. Three forces dominate: 原油是你交易过的最带政治色彩的市场。三股力量主导：

  • OPEC+ — the producer cartel cuts or raises output to defend prices; a surprise decision can gap oil instantly.欧佩克+——产油国联盟通过减产或增产捍卫油价；一次意外决议就能让原油瞬间跳空。
  • Inventories — weekly EIA (US) and API reports reveal supply vs demand; bigger-than-expected builds or draws move WTI hard.库存——每周 EIA（美国）与 API 报告揭示供需；超预期的累库或去库会让 WTI 剧烈波动。
  • Geopolitics — wars, sanctions and shipping chokepoints (Strait of Hormuz) threaten supply and spike prices.地缘政治——战争、制裁、航运咽喉（如霍尔木兹海峡）威胁供应，价格应声飙升。
// Oil gaps on headlines, not on charts. The news calendar is your second chart.原油跟着头条跳空，不跟图表跳空。数据日历就是你的第二张图。

TI.07

What an Index Is指数是什么

Concept概念
One basket, one number — 30 companies become a single price一个篮子一个数字——30 家公司变成唯一的价格

An index is not a company — it is a basket of companies measured as one number. When you trade US30 you are not buying one stock; you are trading the combined value of 30 large US companies. One chart, hundreds of companies inside. 指数不是一家公司，而是一篮子公司打包成一个数字。交易 US30 不是在买某一只股票，而是在交易 30 家美国大公司的合计价值。一张图，里面装着几十上百家公司。

// An index smooths out single-company noise. You trade the economy's mood, not one CEO's earnings call.指数抹平了单家公司的噪音。你交易的是经济的情绪，不是某个 CEO 的财报电话会。

The three move together most of the time — they are all "US stocks" — but with different intensity. NAS100 swings widest because tech earnings and rate expectations hit it hardest. SPX500 is what institutions mean by "the market". US30 reacts less to tech news and more to the broad economy. 三大指数大部分时间同涨同跌——它们都是「美股」——但幅度不同。NAS100 摆动最大，科技财报与利率预期对它的冲击最猛。SPX500 就是机构口中的「大盘」。US30 对科技消息反应小些，更跟随整体经济。

Typical CFD specs — exact sizes and multipliers vary by broker典型 CFD 规格——具体合约大小与乘数因平台而异

Read it this way: one 0.01 move on a standard gold lot is $1; the same move on oil is $10. An identical 50-cent swing is $50 on gold but $500 on oil — same chart, ten times the money. And indices trade on multipliers: a 10-point US30 move at ×10 is $100 per lot. Check the spec sheet before you size, not after. 这样读：标准手黄金每 0.01 美元变动值 $1；原油同样的 0.01 值 $10。同样的 50 美分波动，黄金账户变动 $50，原油账户变动 $500——同一张图，十倍的钱。指数按乘数交易：US30 每点 ×10，10 点波动一手就是 $100。先查合约规格再定仓位，别等亏了才查。

// Pip value and margin are broker-defined. The habit matters more than the number: always check the spec sheet.点值与保证金由平台定义。习惯比数字更重要：永远先看合约规格。
TI.11

Margin: Same Leverage, Different Bite保证金：同样的杠杆，不同的咬合力

Sizing仓位
01

Gold黄金

Moderate point value, relatively smooth moves. A standard lot needs meaningful margin, but daily ranges are usually manageable.点值适中，走势相对平滑。标准手需要的保证金不小，但日常波幅通常可控。

Base sizing 基准仓位
02

Oil原油

Ten times gold's point value per 0.01. A wide-ranging day plus a gap can hit a stop violently — size roughly half of your gold lots.每 0.01 的点值是黄金的十倍。大波动日加上跳空，可以狠狠打穿止损——仓位大约只做黄金的一半。

Half sizing 减半仓位
03

Indices指数

Multiplier contracts carry large notional exposure. At high leverage a 1% index move is a big slice of your account — start with mini lots.乘数合约的名义敞口很大。高杠杆下指数 1% 的波动就是账户的一大块——从小合约起步。

Mini lots 小合约起步

Leverage is the same knob on every instrument — what differs is the notional size per lot. One oil lot or one index lot can move your equity more in a minute than one forex lot does in an hour. When the point value is bigger, the position must be smaller. 杠杆在每个品种上都是同一个旋钮——不同的是每手的名义规模。一手原油或一手指数，一分钟内对账户的冲击可能超过一手外汇一小时。点值越大，仓位必须越小。

// Same 2% rule, smaller lots. The market doesn't know your leverage; your margin call does.还是那条 2% 规则，只是仓位更小。市场不知道你的杠杆，但你的爆仓通知知道。

TI.12

Three Risks That Eat Accounts三个吃账户的风险

Read this必读
  • Oil gaps — weekends, OPEC surprises and war headlines can open oil far beyond your stop; the stop fills, but at a worse price (slippage).原油跳空——周末、欧佩克意外、战争头条能让原油开盘直接越过你的止损；止损会成交，但价格更差（滑点）。
  • Index leverage — one US30 lot can hold tens of thousands of dollars of exposure; at high leverage a 1% index move is a big slice of your account. Size down.指数高杠杆——一手 US30 的名义敞口可达数万美元；高杠杆下指数 1% 的波动就是账户的一大块。仓位要更小。
  • News spikes on metals — NFP, CPI and FOMC can move gold $30+ in minutes; entering without a plan around data is roulette.金属的数据脉冲——非农、CPI、FOMC 能让黄金几分钟波动 30 美元以上；数据前后没有计划就进场等于轮盘赌。
