ICH.01案例 01

What Is Ichimoku什么是一目均衡表

Foundation基础

Ichimoku Kinko Hyo - "one-glance equilibrium chart" - was published in 1969 by Goichi Hosoda after decades of research. It is a complete trading system in one indicator: it shows the trend, its momentum, and the support/resistance zones at the same time. 一目均衡表直译「一眼看清均衡的图表」，由细田悟一研究数十年后于 1969 年发表。它是一个指标里装下一套完整交易系统：趋势、动能、支撑阻力同时呈现。

The system is built from five lines and one cloud: Tenkan-sen (Conversion Line), Kijun-sen (Base Line), Senkou Span A and Senkou Span B (Leading Spans), Chikou Span (Lagging Line) - and the Kumo (cloud) that Span A and Span B draw together. 整套系统由五条线加一片云构成：转换线、基准线、先行带 A / 先行带 B、迟行线——以及由 A、B 两条线共同画出的云。

  • Trend — the cloud tells you the direction and its strength.趋势——云告诉你方向，以及方向有多强。
  • Momentum — the Tenkan/Kijun cross measures the speed of the move.动能——转换线与基准线的交叉衡量这波走得多快。
  • Levels — the cloud edges act as dynamic support and resistance.关键位——云的上下沿就是动态支撑与阻力。
// Ichimoku is not one indicator among many - it is the whole dashboard. Most traders use only 10% of it.一目均衡表不是众多指标之一——它是整块仪表盘。多数人只用到了它 10% 的功能。
ICH.02案例 02

Meet the Five Lines认识五条线

Map地图

Each line has a fixed job. Tenkan-sen and Kijun-sen sit on the price and act like a fast and a slow moving average. Senkou Span A and B are plotted 26 periods into the future and build the cloud. Chikou Span is today's close plotted 26 periods into the past. 每条线各有分工。转换线与基准线叠在价格上，像一快一慢两条均线。先行带 A、B 画在未来 26 期，共同构成云。迟行线则是把今天的收盘价画到过去 26 期的位置。

Nothing here is magic: every line is a midpoint or an average of past prices. That is why Ichimoku is a lagging indicator - it confirms what already happened, and its edge is that it does so in an unusually complete way. 这里没有任何魔法：每条线都是过去价格的中点或平均。所以一目均衡表是滞后指标——它确认已经发生的事，而它的优势在于确认得特别完整。

ICH.03案例 03

The Five Lines in Plain Words五条线的大白话

Intuition直觉

Forget the Japanese names for one minute. Every Ichimoku line answers the same question: what was the middle of the range over the last N candles? Not the average of closes - the middle between the highest high and the lowest low. That is the whole trick.先把日文名字放一边。一目均衡表的每一条线，问的都是同一个问题：过去 N 根 K 线，正中间在哪？注意不是收盘价的平均，是最高点和最低点的正中间。整套系统就建在这一件事上。

Change N and you change the personality of the line. That is the only difference between Tenkan and Kijun.把 N 换个数字，这条线的性格就变了。Tenkan 和 Kijun 的差别，就只有这一个。

Tenkan转换线=(High最高 + Low最低) ÷ 2 · last 9 periods最近 9 期
Tenkan = (High + Low) ÷ 2, last 9 periods转换线 = 最近 9 期（最高 + 最低）÷ 2
  • Tenkan-sen (Conversion Line) — the midpoint of the last 9 periods. It reacts fast and acts like a short-term trendline.转换线——最近 9 期的中点。反应快，相当于短线趋势线。
  • Kijun-sen (Base Line) — the midpoint of the last 26 periods. Slower and stronger; it is the swing's backbone and doubles as a trailing stop.基准线——最近 26 期的中点。更慢、更稳，是波动的脊梁，也常当移动止损用。
  • Senkou Span A (Leading A) — (Tenkan + Kijun) ÷ 2, plotted 26 periods forward. The fast edge of the cloud.先行带 A——（转换线 + 基准线）÷ 2，前移 26 期。云的快沿。
  • Senkou Span B (Leading B) — the midpoint of the last 52 periods, also plotted 26 periods forward. The slow edge of the cloud.先行带 B——最近 52 期的中点，同样前移 26 期。云的慢沿。
  • Chikou Span (Lagging Line) — today's close plotted 26 periods back. It confirms by comparing itself with the price of that time.迟行线——今天的收盘价后移 26 期。通过与当时的价格对比来确认。
// 9 / 26 / 52 are not sacred - they are just the defaults. What matters is that all five lines share the same clock.9 / 26 / 52 不是神圣数字——只是默认值。重要的是五条线共用同一套时钟。

ICH.04案例 04

The Cloud (Kumo)云的含义

The heart核心

Here is the part that confuses everyone, so take it slowly. The cloud is built from two lines you already understand - both are midpoints. Then Ichimoku does something no other indicator does: it takes that band and draws it 26 candles to the right, into empty space where price has not arrived yet.这一段是所有人都会卡住的地方，慢慢看。云是由两条线夹出来的，而那两条线你已经懂了 —— 都是「中点」。接着一目均衡表做了一件别的指标都不做的事：把这条带子整个往右挪 26 根，画到价格还没走到的空白处。

So the cloud ahead of price is not a prediction. It is today's balance zone, shown early. When price finally walks over there, it meets a level that was decided 26 candles ago - which is exactly why so many traders watch it.所以价格前方的那片云不是预测，它是「今天算出来的平衡区，提前摆在那里」。等价格真的走过去，碰到的是 26 根之前就定下的位置 —— 这也正是它会有效的原因：很多人都在看同一条线。

Once that clicks, the rest is just reading position and thickness.这一层想通了，剩下的就只是看两件事：价格站在云的哪一边，以及这片云有多厚。

Three properties do all the work: thickness (how strong the level is), color (who is in control) and position (price above, inside or below the cloud). 真正起作用的是三个属性：厚度（关键位有多强）、颜色（谁在掌控）、位置（价格在云上、云内还是云下）。

  • Thick cloud — many candles overlap, so many traders agree on that price. Support/resistance here is strong; price rarely slides through on the first try.云厚——大量 K 线重叠，说明众多交易者认同那个价格。这里的支撑阻力很强，价格很少第一次就穿过去。
  • Thin cloud — few agreements; price blows through easily. A thin cloud is a warning: breaks here are cheap.云薄——认同少，价格很容易穿过去。薄云是警告：这里的突破很廉价。
  • Color — green when Span A is above Span B (bulls lead); red when Span B is above Span A (bears lead).颜色——A 在 B 上方时云为绿色（多头领先）；B 在 A 上方时为红色（空头领先）。
  • Twist — where A and B cross, the cloud changes color: a potential turning zone. Price arriving at the cloud edge at the same moment is one of the strongest Ichimoku setups.扭转——A 与 B 交叉处云会变色：那是潜在转折区。若价格同时到达云沿，就是一目均衡表最强形态之一。
// A thick green cloud under price = a rising floor. A thick red cloud above price = a falling ceiling.价格下方厚而绿的云 = 不断抬升的地板；价格上方厚而红的云 = 不断压低的天花板。

ICH.05案例 05

Tenkan / Kijun Cross金叉与死叉

Signal信号

When Tenkan crosses above Kijun, short-term momentum has turned up - a golden cross. Crossing below is a death cross. On its own this is a mediocre signal that whipsaws in ranges; the cloud turns it into a filter. 转换线上穿基准线，短线动能转多——金叉；下穿则是死叉。单独看这是个平庸的信号，震荡市里来回抽；云把它变成了过滤器。

✕ What beginners do新手常犯
  1. See a golden cross and buy at market看到金叉就市价买入
  2. No cloud check, no direction check不看云、不辨方向
  3. Cross fires inside or against the cloud - stop hit交叉发生在云内或逆着云——止损被打
→ A cross without the cloud is a coin flip.
没有云的交叉，跟抛硬币没区别。
✓ What works正确做法
  1. Golden cross ABOVE a green cloud = long with the trend绿色云上方的金叉 = 顺势做多
  2. Death cross BELOW a red cloud = short with the trend红色云下方的死叉 = 顺势做空
  3. Cross inside the cloud = skip; wait for the edge or a break云内的交叉 = 跳过，等云沿或突破
→ Cross + cloud direction + cloud position = edge.
交叉 + 云的方向 + 云的位置 = 优势。
// Kijun-sen doubles as your trailing stop: in an uptrend, price holding above it keeps the long alive.基准线还能当移动止损：上升趋势里，价格守在它上方，多单就继续持有。

ICH.06案例 06

Chikou Confirmation迟行线确认

Underused被低估

Chikou is today's close drawn 26 periods back. By comparing it with the price that actually traded there, you get the market's own verdict: if Chikou stands above the historical price, buyers were right - bullish confirmation. Below it, bearish. 迟行线是今天的收盘价画到 26 期之前。拿它和当时真实成交的价格对比，就得到市场自己的证词：迟行线站在历史价格上方，说明买方是对的——看多确认；在下方，则看空。

Use it as the last filter: direction from the cloud, trigger from the cross, and only then check whether Chikou agrees. When all three line up, the setup carries the full weight of the system. 把它当最后一道过滤器：方向看云，扳机看交叉，最后再确认迟行线是否同意。三者一致时，这个形态背后有整套系统的重量。

  • Chikou above history — the recent move is confirmed by the market; longs are in harmony with the system.迟行线在历史价格上方——这波上涨得到市场确认，做多与系统同频。
  • Chikou below history — shorts are in harmony.迟行线在历史价格下方——做空与系统同频。
  • Crossing right now — the verdict is changing; wait for a close above or below to confirm.正在穿越——证词刚翻页；等收盘站稳再确认。
// Chikou is the only line that asks the market itself - not an average - whether the move is real.五条线里只有迟行线在问市场本人——而不是某个平均——这波是不是真的。

ICH.07案例 07

The Three States三种状态

Context first先看背景

Before any cross, before any entry: where is price relative to the cloud? This one question replaces half of your other indicators. 任何交叉、任何进场之前，先问：价格相对于云在哪个位置？这一个问题就能替代你一半的其他指标。

Above the Cloud云上 · 多头

Price above the cloud = the bulls own the session. The cloud below acts as a rising support floor. Trade longs; treat every dip toward the cloud as a candidate entry.价格在云上 = 多头掌控。下方的云是不断抬升的支撑地板。只做多，每次回踩到云附近都是候选买点。

Inside the Cloud云内 · 震荡

Inside the cloud = equilibrium, indecision, chop. The system has no verdict here. Stand aside; wait for a close outside the cloud before trading again.价格在云内 = 均衡、犹豫、拉锯。系统在这里没有裁决。观望，等收盘站出云外再动手。

Below the Cloud云下 · 空头

Price below the cloud = the bears own the session. The cloud above acts as a falling resistance ceiling. Trade shorts; treat every rally toward the cloud as a candidate entry.价格在云下 = 空头掌控。上方的云是不断压低的天花板。只做空，每次反弹到云附近都是候选卖点。

// The state comes first, the signal second. A perfect cross in the wrong state is still a losing trade.先定状态，后看信号。状态不对，再完美的交叉也是一笔亏损单。

ICH.08案例 08

Common Mistakes三个烧钱的错误

Read this必读
  • 1 · Trading the cross without the cloud — a Tenkan/Kijun cross is noise until you check where it happens. Inside or against the cloud it fails constantly.① 只看交叉不看云——不先确认交叉发生在哪里，金叉死叉就是噪音。在云内或逆着云，它经常失效。
  • 2 · Ignoring the timeframe — Ichimoku is a swing system; on M1–M5 the lines and the cloud whip around so fast that every signal looks fake. H1 and above is its home.② 忽视时间框架——一目均衡表是波段系统；在 M1–M5 上，五条线和云转得飞快，每个信号都像假的。H1 及以上才是它的主场。
  • 3 · Trading inside the cloud — the cloud is the no-man's-land. Fading its edges is a strategy; entering inside it is paying spread for random outcomes.③ 在云内频繁交易——云是无人区。在云沿做反转是有策略；钻进云里交易，只是付着点差赌随机结果。
// Every misuse above shares one root cause: asking the system for a signal before reading the cloud it drew.以上误用只有一个根源：还没读懂云，就先向系统要信号。
