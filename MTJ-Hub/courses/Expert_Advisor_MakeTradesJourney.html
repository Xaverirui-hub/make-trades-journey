What is an EA?

01
Automation自动化

E.01

EA = Expert AdvisorEA 就是专家顾问

Definition定义

An Expert Advisor (EA) is an automated trading program that runs inside MetaTrader 5. You write your trading logic in code; the EA watches the market for you and opens, manages and closes trades on its own. It works on gold (XAUUSD), forex and indices — anything MT5 can chart. 专家顾问（EA）是跑在 MetaTrader 5 里的自动交易程式。你把交易逻辑写进代码，EA 替你盯盘，自动开单、管理、平仓。黄金（XAUUSD）、外汇、指数都支援 —— 只要 MT5 能显示图表的东西，它都能交易。

The logic is written into the code as fixed rules: entry, exit and risk control all execute automatically. Nothing is decided "in the moment" — because no human is there to decide it. 逻辑以固定规则写死在代码里：进场、出场、风控全部自动执行。没有任何决定是「当下才拍板的」—— 因为当下根本没有人类在拍板。

// EA 是「Expert Advisor」的缩写。它跑在 MT5 里面，不是独立软件，也不是什么神秘的黑盒子。
// An EA is not magic software. It is the automation of what you already do by hand. EA不是神奇软件，它只是把「你本来用手做的事」自动化。
E.02

Like Autopilot, Not Like Magic像自动驾驶，不是魔法

Analogy类比

Think of an EA like autopilot on a plane: you decide the route, the altitude and the rules — the machine flies them precisely. It does not invent a new route because it "feels like" turning left. 把 EA 想成飞机的自动驾驶：路线、高度、规则都是你定的 —— 机器只是精确地执行。它不会因为「感觉想左转」就发明一条新路线。

The rules are yours. The discipline is the machine's. That division of labour is the entire point of an EA. 规则是你的，纪律是机器的。这种分工，就是 EA 的全部意义。

Part 02第二部分

Why Use an EA?

为 什 么 用 E A
02
The edge优势所在

Five Things a Human Can't Beat人类赢不了的五件事

E.03

No Emotions无情绪

Benefit好处

No greed to hold a winner too long. No fear to cut a loser too early. No revenge trading after a loss. The rules are written in code, so psychology costs nothing — the EA never feels the trade. 不会贪心而抱单太久，不会恐惧而过早砍仓，亏损后不会报复性交易。规则写死在代码里，心理成本为零 —— EA 对这笔交易没有任何感觉。

Most blown accounts are not blown by bad analysis. They are blown by the trader doing something in the middle of a losing streak. An EA removes that whole category of mistakes. 大多数爆仓的帐户不是死于分析差，而是死于交易者在连亏途中「做了一些事」。EA 直接把这一整类错误删掉了。

E.04

No Screen Time无需盯盘

Benefit好处

An EA runs 7×24. When a signal appears at 3 a.m., it trades — you are asleep, and that is fine. When the London open breaks out while you are at work, it is already in the trade. EA 是 7×24 小时运行的。凌晨三点信号出现，它照做 —— 你在睡觉，没关系。你在上班时伦敦开盘突破，它已经进场了。

Missed opportunities are a silent cost that almost nobody tracks. The EA's job is to make sure "I wasn't watching" is never the reason a trade did not happen. 错过的机会是一笔几乎没人统计的隐性成本。EA 的工作，就是确保「我没在看盘」永远不会成为没交易的理由。

E.05

Discipline严格纪律

Benefit好处

No itchy fingers. No hesitation. No "let me wait for one more candle". When the condition is true, the order goes in — every single time, exactly the same way. 不手痒、不犹豫、不说「再看一根 K 线」。条件一满足就下单 —— 每一次都一样，一模一样。

Discipline is the rarest skill in trading, and it is the one skill that does not require you to have it — if you hand the job to a machine that was never tempted. 纪律是交易里最稀缺的能力，而它恰好是唯一「不需要你本人拥有」的能力 —— 只要你把工作交给一个永远不会被诱惑的机器。

E.06

Speed速度

Benefit好处

Milliseconds. When a breakout happens or news hits, the window can be seconds wide. A human needs to see the chart, process it, decide, click, confirm — the EA reacts in milliseconds. 毫秒级。突破发生或数据公布的瞬间，机会窗口可能只有几秒。人要看到图表、思考、决定、点击、确认 —— EA 是毫秒级反应。

This matters most in fast markets: high-impact data releases, London / NY opens, breakout moments. 这在快速行情中最关键：重要数据公布、伦敦/纽约开盘、突破瞬间。

E.07

Consistency一致性

Benefit好处

The same signal is handled the same way every time. Trade #100 gets the same treatment as trade #1 — same entry, same stop, same size, same exit. 同一个信号永远用同样的方式处理。第 100 笔和第 1 笔待遇完全相同 —— 一样的进场、一样的止损、一样的手数、一样的出场。

This is what makes your results measurable. If every trade is executed identically, then your win rate, your R:R and your expectancy are real numbers — not guesses about what you "usually" do. 正因为如此，你的结果才可以被测量。如果每笔交易都执行得一模一样，你的胜率、盈亏比、期望值就是真实的数字 —— 而不是「我大概都这样做」的猜测。

// Put together: No Emotions + No Screen Time + Discipline + Speed + Consistency = a trader who never has a bad day.合起来就是：无情绪 + 无需盯盘 + 纪律 + 速度 + 一致性 = 一个永远不会「状态不好」的交易者。

What an EA Does NOT Do

E A 不 是 万 能
03
Myths破除迷思

It's a Tool, Not a Money Printer它是工具，不是印钞机

E.08

It Does NOT Guarantee Profit不保证赚钱

Reality现实

An EA with bad logic loses money exactly as reliably as a human with bad logic — it just does it faster and with perfect discipline. 逻辑很差的 EA 亏钱，和逻辑很差的人亏钱一样可靠 —— 只是亏得更快、亏得更有纪律。

If the rules behind it have no edge, automation does not create an edge. It only amplifies whatever is already in the rules — good or bad. 如果规则本身没有优势，自动化并不会创造优势。它只会放大规则里本来就有的一切 —— 无论好坏。

// EA 只是工具，不是印钞机。逻辑差，它照样亏。 An EA is a tool, not a money printer. Bad logic loses money either way.
E.09

It Is NOT AI它不是 AI

Reality现实

An EA does not think, learn or adapt. It executes exactly the rules you wrote — nothing more. If the market changes and your rules stop working, the EA will keep following them happily into the drawdown. EA 不会思考、不会学习、不会适应。它只执行你写好的规则 —— 仅此而已。如果市场变了、规则失效了，EA 会高高兴兴地照着旧规则一路亏进回撤里。

Adapting to new conditions is your job. The EA is the soldier; you are the general. 适应新行情是你的工作。EA 是士兵，你才是将军。

E.10

It Is NOT Set-and-Forget不是放着不管

Reality现实

An EA still needs a human: to set the parameters correctly, to monitor that it behaves as expected, and to verify the results. A machine running unattended with wrong parameters is just a machine losing money unattended. EA 仍然需要人：正确地设定参数、监控它是否如预期运作、验证结果。一台参数错误的机器无人看管，就只是一台无人看管的亏钱机器。

E.11

The EA Decision LoopEA 的决策循环

Overview总览

Every EA, no matter how complicated, is built from the same four decisions. Understand these four and you understand any EA you will ever see. 无论多复杂的 EA，都由这同一个四个决定组成。看懂这四个，你就看懂了你以后会遇到的任何 EA。

E.12

The Four Components四大组成

Anatomy结构
PART 01

Entry Condition

进 场 条 件

The signal that opens a trade — e.g. "when the MA fast line crosses above the slow line, buy". No signal, no trade.

Signal → Open position
PART 02

Exit Condition

出 场 条 件

When the trade ends: stop loss, take profit, or a trailing stop that follows the price.

Stop / Target / Trail
PART 03

Position Sizing

仓 位 管 理

How big each trade is — fixed lots, or risk-based sizing so every trade risks the same % of your balance.

Lots = f(risk %)
PART 04

Risk Control

风 险 控 制

The circuit breakers: max daily loss, max open positions, max spread — the rules that keep a bad day small.

Caps & Limits

E.13

Step 1 — Trade Manually First第一步：先学会手动交易

Foundation基础

You cannot automate rules you do not have. The first 21 lessons of MTJ exist precisely to build this foundation: entries, exits, risk, psychology. Manual trading is the prerequisite. 没有规则，就无法自动化。MTJ 前 21 课存在的目的就是打这个基础：进场、出场、风控、心理。先会手动交易，是前提。

E.14

Step 2 — Write the Rules Down第二步：把规则写清楚

Foundation基础

What signal opens a trade? What condition closes it? What is the max risk per trade? If you cannot write a rule in one clear sentence, the EA cannot execute it either. 什么信号进场？什么情况出场？单笔最大风险是多少？如果你不能把一条规则写成一个清楚的句子，EA 也没办法执行它。

  • Entry — the exact signal that opens the trade进场 —— 触发开单的那个确切信号
  • Exit — stop, target, trailing rules出场 —— 止损、止盈、追踪规则
  • Risk — % per trade, max daily loss风控 —— 单笔风险%、每日最大亏损
E.15

Step 3 — Automate the Logic第三步：把逻辑自动化

MTJ Tool配套工具

If your rules are clear enough to write down, they are clear enough to compose. 规则清楚到写得出来，就清楚到拼得出来。

E.16

Step 4 — Verify Before You Trust第四步：验证，再信任

The rule铁律

The order is non-negotiable: backtest → demo → live. Never skip a stage. 顺序没有商量余地：回测 → 模拟 → 实盘。一步都不能跳。

✓ Backtest回测
  1. Run the EA on years of historical data让 EA 跑多年历史数据
  2. Check win rate, drawdown, expectancy检查胜率、回撤、期望值
  3. Only continue if the numbers hold up数字过关才继续
✓ Demo模拟
  1. Run it live on a demo account for weeks在模拟帐户上实况跑几周
  2. Watch for execution issues, spread, slippage观察执行问题、点差、滑点
  3. Check it matches the backtest确认与回测结果一致
// Only after backtest and demo both pass do you consider a small live account. Start small.回测和模拟都通过之后，才考虑小额实盘。从小开始。
